How to use this desk · tags, prices and league assumptions

Record, don’t guess

Search the nominated player. Choose the winning team and enter the actual price. Empty bids never become $1 automatically. Tap a player name for details, your note, a personal stop and the Oct 18 news snapshot (status + source link). On a phone, use Scout for the bid guide and Record for the sale form.

Switching devices: saves stay in this browser, not on Fantrax or your other devices. Export a backup here, transfer it and import it on your phone to continue. Keep one device as the active ledger to avoid conflicting copies.

One pricing authority

All 229 players, targets and default stops are copied unchanged from the war room. Inflation is that board’s heuristic—not a market quote or permission to exceed your stop. Current NBA news and individual game schedules have not been independently re-verified. Open league rules (PDF).

Read the decision, not a code

✓ Like: buy at the right price. Anchors are the five premium players; Stars are the other top-15 core targets; Targets are the rest of your core; Value means a Fantrax blindspot. △ Caution: traps, playoff risks and recovery stashes—not an automatic ban. ⊘ Avoid: DND or your personal pass. Upside and depth are optional.

★ My pick is your saved shortlist, separate from the Star tier. Favouriting a DND player never turns him into a recommendation. Original tags and projections remain unchanged in details.

14 picks, not 15

Rules p1: every team drafts 14 players total. “Plan IR” still uses one of those picks and its salary. The extra IR spot applies during the season only; an eligible player is suspended, out or IR-tagged. Once eligibility ends, you have 48 hours to remove him or other transactions are blocked. The desk’s IR label is a plan, not a Fantrax status check.

Keepers and money

Up to 3 keepers; next salary = current salary × 1.05, rounded up. Examples: $1→$2, $10→$11, $30→$32, $50→$53. Future draft buying room above $1 minimums = $200 + net traded draft capital + any consolation bonus − keeper salaries − (14 − keeper count). This desk tracks tonight’s draft only, not future keeper eligibility or trades.

Draft capital resets after the draft. In-season FA has its own separate $200 budget; saving draft cash does not increase it.

Calendar and scoring

Trade deadline: Feb 14, 2027, 11:59 p.m. EST. Playoffs and FA/keeper lock: Mar 15. Top 4 advance; two rounds of two weeks give Mar 15–28 and Mar 29–Apr 11. Three FA claims weekly, processed Mon/Wed/Fri at 5 a.m. EST.
